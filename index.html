⚖️ Weight Settings (calculation)

Calculated weight = (wire + sheet) × (1 + wastage%)
• Wire = 25.4/(opening+dia) × dia² × 0.04542 × area(sqft)  (2.46 kg/sqft type)
• Sheet = area(sqft) × Sheet mm × factor  (solid steel ≈ 0.73, perforated kam — default 0.44)
• Item me "Sheet mm" bharoge tabhi sheet weight judega. Example: 22×8, 1780×1550, sheet 1.2 → wire 365.46 + sheet 78.4 = 443.85 kg (5 pc). 5%/15% wastage chahiye to upar daal do.
